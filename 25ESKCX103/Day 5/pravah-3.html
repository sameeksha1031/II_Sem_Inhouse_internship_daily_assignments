The Home Ground

About SKIT

Swami Keshvanand Institute of Technology, Management & Gramothan (SKIT), Jaipur, is one of Rajasthan's oldest private engineering colleges — a campus that's spent over two decades building engineers by day and, every July, turning into a festival ground by night. PRAVAH is SKIT's flagship fest: the one week a year when labs empty out, the open grounds fill up, and every department shows up to outdo the other.
